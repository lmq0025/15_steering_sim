    10: /* *******************************************************************************
    11: TITLE : PU001
    12: ------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    13: DESCRIPTION :
    14: 	
    15: 	PU001 is a submodel of an ideal hydraulic pump. There are no flow
    16: 	losses or mechanical losses and the flow rate is determines solely
    17: 	by the shaft speed, pump displacement and the inlet port pressure 
    18: 	(normally port 1).
    19: 	
    20: 	If there is air release or cavitation at the inlet port, the flow
    21: 	rate will be reduced.
    22:  
    23:         When the pump is rotated backwards, the port that is nominally the
    24:         outlet (port 2) becomes the inlet. Under these conditions the
    25:         pressure at port 2 is used in determining the flow rate. To avoid
    26:         a discontinuity, a smooth switch between the pressures is used. The
    27:         parameter typical speed of pump (wtyp) is used to determine the
    28:         speed range over which the transmission is made. The switching between
    29:         the two pressures is assumed to take place between speeds + and - 
    30:         wtyp/1000.	
    31: ------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    32: USAGE :
    33:  
    34: 	This submodel is useful for general simulation of a fixed displacement
    35: 	hydraulic pump.
    36: ------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    37: PARAMETER SETTINGS :
    38:  
    39: 	The typical pump speed need not be accurate -- it is just required
    40: 	to give a rough idea of the speed at which the pump shaft will be
    41: 	turning. FOR ALMOST ALL APPLICATION THE DEFAULT VALUE IS ACCEPTABLE.
    42: 	CHANGE THE PARAMETER ONLY IF THE PUMP SPEED IS ABNORMALLY FAST 
    43: 	(> 5000 REV/MIN) OR ABNORMALLY SLOW (< 100 REV/MIN).
    44: 
    45: 	The index of hydraulic fluid parameter refers to a fluid type
    46: 	defined by an icon put on the sketch.
