    10: /* *******************************************************************************
    11:  
    12: TITLE : MAS004
    13: ------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    14: DESCRIPTION :
    15:  
    16:    A mass with 2 ports which is capable of one-dimensional motion
    17:    under a external forces applied to the ports and frictional forces.
    18:    Absolute value of inertial power is integrated and gives inertial activity.
    19:    Absolute value of the power dissipated in the friction is integrated and
    20:    gives dissipative activity.
    21:  
    22: ------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    23: USAGE :
    24:  
    25:    MAS004 differs from MAS002 only in the inclusion of friction.
    26:  
    27: ------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    28: PARAMETER SETTINGS :
    29:  
    30:    The user must define friction characteristics as follows:
    31:  
    32:         the coefficient of viscous friction (N/(m/s)),
    33:         the coefficient of windage (N/(m/s)**2),
    34:         the Couloumb friction force (N),
    35:         the stiction force (N).
    36:  
    37:    None of these quantities may be negative. The stiction force is the
    38:    maximum friction force when the mass is at rest and the Coulomb
    39:    friction force is the friction force (assumed constant) when the
    40:    mass is in motion. Note that the stiction level cannot be less than
    41:    the Coulomb friction. If the stiction force is a lot bigger than
    42:    the Coulomb friction force the motion can be very `jerky' but such
    43:    motion does often occur.
    44:  
    45:    Both viscous friction (proportional to speed) and windage
    46:    (proportional to speed squared) add damping resulting in a steady
    47:    speed being reached quicker.  The inclination of the load (degree)
    48:    should be in the range -90 to +90. A value of +90 indicates that
    49:    port 1 is at the lowest point.
    50:  
    51: ------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    52: DATE OF CREATION/AUTHOR : 11/02/97 C.W.Richards
    53: ------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    54: INDEX OF REVISIONS :
    55: ------------------------------------------------------------------------------
